# Awesome-Construction-Field-Management

## Top Construction Field Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Daily Reports, Task Management, Plan Viewing, Safety, Time Tracking, Site Coordination & Jobsite Productivity*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Construction Field Management**. These tools help superintendents, foremen, and field teams capture daily reports, manage tasks and issues on plans, track time and production, conduct safety inspections, and keep the office connected to the jobsite in real time.



**Examples** include Raken, Fieldwire, HammerTech, Safesite, Procore, Autodesk Build, SiteMax, Assignar, eSUB, and BusyBusy (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for site management, field service, construction ERP modules, and jobsite coordination — ideal for contractors, GCs, and developers seeking self-hosted or highly customizable alternatives.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[SiteWise](https://github.com/Site-Wise/app)**  

  Open-source, mobile-first, self-hostable construction site management app for tracking materials, labour, expenses, vendor bills, site progress, and multi-site operations with offline support.



- **[OpenConstructionERP](https://github.com/datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionERP)**  

  Comprehensive open-source construction ERP covering BOQ, CAD/BIM takeoff, cost databases, 4D/5D scheduling, daily diaries, HSE, punch lists, and field modules.



- **[Construbot](https://github.com/javier-llamas/construbot)**  

  Open-source construction management system focused on contracts, milestones, estimates, progress billing, financial tracking, and multi-tenancy.



- **[FieldOpt](https://github.com/zblauser/fieldopt)**  

  Open-source web-based field management and job organization system with technician assignment, skill-based matching, location tracking, and job state management.



- **[ERPNext Construction / Frappe](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Fully open-source ERP with project, inventory, and construction-oriented modules that can be extended for field reporting, material tracking, and site management.



- **[Odoo Community (Project + Field Service)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Modular open-source ERP whose Project, Field Service, Inventory, and Timesheet apps are commonly adapted for construction field operations.



- **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**  

  Mature open-source project management platform with Gantt, work packages, and collaboration features suitable for construction project and field coordination.



- **[FlexDesk](https://github.com/Paulo-BatistaFerraz/flexdesk)**  

  Open-source field service management platform with job scheduling, technician assignment, invoicing, offline support, and GPS features adaptable to construction crews.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Daily reporting & forms**: Custom form builders and mobile data collection tools (e.g., based on KoboToolbox or ODK) used on jobsites.

- **Plan & drawing tools**: Open-source PDF annotation and markup libraries that can power field plan viewing.

- **Time & attendance**: Self-hosted time tracking systems with GPS and cost-code support.

- **Safety & inspections**: Community templates and mobile apps for checklists and observation reporting.

- **BIM / model coordination**: FreeCAD, BlenderBIM, and related open BIM tools that complement field management.

- Many regional and research prototypes for **site diaries**, **punch lists**, and **resource allocation** on GitHub.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Use **SiteWise** or **OpenConstructionERP** for dedicated site and field operations, layer **ERPNext/Odoo** for financial and resource integration, adopt **OpenProject** for overall project coordination, and combine with mobile form tools for inspections and daily reports. Offline-first design and simple mobile UX remain critical for field adoption.

## Disclaimer

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require reliable mobile connectivity strategies, data backup, user training, and integration with existing accounting/PM systems for production use.
